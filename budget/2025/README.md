# Cardano Budget 2025

> _The road to hell is paved with good intentions._

The current Cardano budget process is, in a word, chaos. Since unveiling in
August 2024 the budget process has been subject to frequent changes and pivots,
bureaucratic and technical delays, and an overall lack of clarity that has
materially harmed confidence in the process.

This culminated, most recently, in "The Great Pivot" of February 2025 when the
process again changed, without consultation of Intersect membership. Nothing
more poignantly reflects the confusion and uncertainty of the moment than
proposals from both TSC and IOG for "Core Development".

The key difference (aside from a handful of estimated FTEs) between TSC and IOG
proposals is their proposed process. IOG contains pre-selected/screened partner
developers whereas TSC proposes a public tendering process, the details of which
are currently lacking.

IOG proposed process is similar to that of a general contractor that has been
conducted over the last 7+ years of Cardano core development and leverages many
known and trusted names in the ecosystem as partners and subcontractors to
assist in the delivery.

TSC proposes a public tendering/RFP contracting process that is common in the
distribution of public funding and aligning with the original Intersect Budget
Process of Q4 '24. While this is admirable, it has a fatal flaw: too much trust
in Intersect to execute and too little time.

Ignoring, for a moment, the fact that Intersect does not currently have an RFP
process that is efficient or sufficient to the task... A rotating, elected body
of 9 individuals can optimistically manage issuance, awarding, and oversight of
potentially 2-10 RFPs per year.

This is evidenced by the Continuity and Maintenance Contract between Intersect
and IOG that has been in negotiation, with TSC consultation, since Dec. '24 and
is, as yet, unexecuted and rapidly nearing the end of its proposed Q1-Q2 2025
time period.

The gears of bureaucracy grind slowly and, to be blunt, Intersect has not had
enough time, appropriate leadership, demonstrated ability/capacity, or the
explicit mandate of the larger Cardano community to serve this function on their
behalf (if even desired).

Respectfully, I must disagree with my colleagues on the TSC that their chosen
method of execution is the right choice for Cardano, particularly for the 2025
budget year. I have much faith and respect for my fellow committee members but
no confidence for the execution.

My lack of faith stems from lack of strong leadership (servant or otherwise) and
vision setting from the Intersect executive leadership team. Committee mandates
are unclear, processes change rapidly, and questions are met with bureaucratic
bloviation rather than answers.

We are nearly 30% of the way through 2025 and still have yet to see transparent
fiscal reporting on behalf of Intersect for 2024... Yet we are expected to trust
them to execute on administration, auditing, and oversight of hundreds of
millions of funds per year?

Yet, the only greater danger is that we may do nothing and see the Cardano
ecosystem flounder. We clearly have no shortage of talented and eager companies
looking to develop and build on behalf of and within the ecosystem. So, what are
we to do about it?

Sufficiently large and trusted entities (IOG, Catalyst, CF) should submit
individual, standalone budgets to the community with independent administration
and auditing provisions for public transparency and accountability. We don't
need additional middlemen.

Intersect MUST provide clear, transparent financial reporting for their 2024
expenses and relative levels of engaged and managed projects (RFPs) so the
community may judge and determine their capability to execute on 2025 and future
budgets.

Intersect budget SHOULD be managed and overseen by the committees but SHOULD
only cover the specific mission and scope of Intersect as an organization rather
than attempt to capture and control the financial access of the entire Cardano
ecosystem.

Intersect and other budgets SHOULD BE as lean as possible to continue and
progress work in 2025 while the community has time to further refine and build
our budgetary systems and processes for 2026 and beyond. We need to keep pushing
forward, responsibly.

ALL BUDGETS MUST contain provisions to avoid double-spending for work otherwise
compensated through Treasury sources and MUST include provisions that
unallocated funds will be returned to the Treasury upon expiration of the
budget's applicable period.

ALL BUDGETS MUST include detailed information regarding their transparency and
audit processes. A single Administrator SHOULD NOT BE responsible for more than
33% of the Cardano ecosystem annual Net Change Limit. These are my opinions, do
with them what you will.
